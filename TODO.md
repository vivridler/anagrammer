# Crossword Solver – to-do

Ideas and fixes for the crossword app (`crossword/`). Newest at the bottom.

- [ ] **Pencil mode** – move the ✎ (edit clue) button to the top bar and put a pencil toggle in its place on
  the clue bar. In pencil mode, letters are entered in a lighter colour, for tentative answers.
- [ ] **Less padding around the clue-bar buttons**, so the clue text doesn't wrap so much.
- [ ] **Colour below the keyboard** – the strip under the keyboard (the phone's home-bar area) should match
  the keyboard colour.
- [ ] **Answers spread over several grid entries** (e.g. 24-across + 2-down, 23-down + 1-across) – treat them
  as one answer: highlight all parts together, carry typing from one part into the next, and make
  "See 24-across" entries go to the full clue.
- [ ] **Word breaks in the grid** – use the lengths in brackets to mark where words end in multi-word answers:
  a thicker divider for word breaks, e.g. (5,2), and a hyphen mark for hyphens, e.g. (5-4,6).
- [ ] **Timer** – time how long the puzzle is open, from starting to solve until the crossword is finished.
